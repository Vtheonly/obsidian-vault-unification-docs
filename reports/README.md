# Reports Directory

Store generated evidence reports here. Reports must be reproducible where possible and must distinguish measured facts from interpretation.

Recommended report families:
- `inventory/`: source inventory, hashes, file-type counts, asset and extraction failures.
- `coverage/`: source-unit accounting, valid destinations, semantic validation coverage.
- `verification/`: deterministic, lexical, structural, semantic, technical, and editorial audit results.
- `findings/`: actionable findings and repair outcomes.
- `regression/`: baseline comparisons and regression test outcomes.
- `release/`: final acceptance evidence and limitations.

Do not store a claim such as “100% complete” without a defined denominator, method, evidence, and limitation statement. Keep reports out of ordinary final-vault notes.
