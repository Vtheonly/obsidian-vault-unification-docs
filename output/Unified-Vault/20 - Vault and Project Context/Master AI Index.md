This note archives the collection's alphabetical index as it stood at the end of iteration 1: a stub, seeded with a partial list of concept notes, that was scheduled to be filled in during iteration 2. It is preserved as project context rather than live navigation — the collection's navigation runs through its topical maps of content, with [[Master AI MOC]] as the master map — and it records both the plan for completing the index and every seed entry exactly as archived.

## The stub and its population plan

The index was meant to become an alphabetical index of every concept note in the vault. At iteration 1 it was a stub, to be populated in iteration 2; until then, readers were pointed to the [[Master AI MOC]] for navigation. The record was created on 2026-08-07 as part of iteration 1 and carried the tags index and navigation. The population plan, kept as written: to populate the index in iteration 2, run a script that walks the vault and lists every note by title, with the script saved under `scripts/` per the script-persistence rule.

## Reading the seed list

The seed list mixes three kinds of entry: plain links to notes that existed at the time, entries marked "(planned)" for notes that had not yet been written, and section-path entries that pointed to a note by its location rather than its title. Below, every entry is linked to its corresponding note in this vault; path-form entries are rewritten to their targets, and the archived status markers and placements are kept exactly.

The archived list has quirks that are flagged in place rather than fixed: some path-form entries sit under letters that do not match their titles, the self-attention note was entered twice with the path-form copy redirecting to the plain entry, the QLoRA entry under P is a cross-reference to its filing under Q, and the static-embeddings note appears three times — a planned section-path entry under E, a planned plain entry under V, and an unmarked entry under W. Letters with no entries are simply absent, except Z, which the list records as empty.

## Why the stub survives

As archived, the index never advanced past its stub — navigation settled on the topical maps of content, and the flat alphabetical list was left as a snapshot. That snapshot is the record's value: it shows which concept notes existed at the end of iteration 1, which were still planned, and how sparsely the alphabet was filled, with no entries at all under H, I, J, O, U, X or Y and none under Z. For live navigation, start from [[Master AI MOC]] or follow the [[Learning Roadmap]]; the [[Reading Orders]] collect the goal-tailored alternatives.
